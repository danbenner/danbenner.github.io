Help / FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Portly stores meal history, saved meals, recipes, nutrition details, and photos locally on your device. No account is required.

How do I remove my data?

Settings includes separate actions for clearing meal history, saved meals, and recipes. Clearing data cannot be undone.

How does recipe import work?

Premium can import a recipe from a PDF or a photo you select or capture. Review imported text before relying on it.

Can I export recipes?

Yes. From Settings, choose a folder and Portly will copy or create PDFs for your saved recipes.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the subscription.

Is Portly medical advice?

No. Nutrition totals and food-preference tools are organizational aids and are not medical diagnosis or treatment.
