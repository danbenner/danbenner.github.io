DigiDrop Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

DigiDrop ("we," "us," or "our") is designed to keep gameplay local and simple. This policy explains what the app stores, how it uses that information, and what it does not do.

Data We Collect

  • We do not collect personal information, account data, or gameplay history on our servers.
  • The app may hold your current round state, menu choices, and score locally while you play.
  • If you contact support, we will only use the information you choose to send us.

How We Use Data

  • To run the game on your device.
  • To show the current round, score, timer, and selected settings.
  • To display support, privacy, and app information pages you open from the app.

Local Use Only

DigiDrop does not require an account and does not use CloudKit sync. The app is intended to work as a local, on-device game.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not intentionally send game data to third parties.

Data Sharing

The app only shares data when you choose to open Apple-provided system features, such as the App Store review flow, or when you send information through the support contact page.

Contact

For privacy or legal questions, use the support contact listed for DigiDrop in the App Store or the support page linked from this site.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When that happens, we will update the effective date above.
