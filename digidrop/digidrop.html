How DigiDrop plays

The app is built around short sessions, so the menu and gameplay stay simple and fast.

1

Choose your setup

Set the difficulty, starting time, and math operation from the menu before the round starts.

2

Build the answer

Tap numbers on the board and work toward the target value using the selected operation.

3

Beat the clock

Keep an eye on the timer and score while you try to finish as many rounds as you can.

4

Use support when needed

The app links to FAQ, privacy, and bug reporting so help is always close by.

Built for short bursts

DigiDrop is meant to be a quick game you can start and stop without extra setup or account work.

Local by design

The app keeps its experience on-device and uses hosted pages only for privacy and support links.
