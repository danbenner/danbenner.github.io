Help / FAQ

These are the most likely questions from the menu, gameplay, and settings flow.

How do I start a game?

Choose a difficulty, a starting time, and a math operation from the main menu, then start a round.

Can I change settings later?

Yes. Adjust difficulty, time, or operation from the menu before you begin a new round.

Where is my progress stored?

DigiDrop keeps gameplay local to the device and does not use an online account or cloud save.

How do I send a bug report?

Use the support email above or the hosted support link that is available from the app.
