Help / FAQ

Where is my workout data stored?

Loaf stores plans, custom training, workout sessions, settings, and progress photos locally on your device. No account is required.

How do I start training?

Choose a plan, open a workout routine, pick a single exercise, or use Quick Walk or Quick Run from the workout home screen.

Can I use equipment workouts?

Premium lets you turn off Body Weight Only and choose the equipment available to you for library filtering and training.

How do I remove my data?

Settings includes separate actions for clearing workout history, custom workouts, custom exercises, and progress photos.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the subscription.

Is Loaf medical advice?

No. Loaf is a workout organization tool. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new program when appropriate.
