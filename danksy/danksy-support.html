Help / FAQ

These are the common questions that come up when people first use the prompt, gallery, and tracing tools.

Where is my art stored?

Danksy keeps your art and prompt data locally on your device unless you export it yourself.

How do I export art?

Open Settings, go to Art Export, and choose a folder. Danksy will create a dated export folder for you.

Can I clear data?

Yes. Settings includes clear-data actions for removing local art and prompt history from the device.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. Danksy is designed to work as a local-first art tool.
