What NewLeaf tracks

Everything is grouped into a simple flow so you can log what matters without getting lost in a heavy interface.

Nutrition

Meal and macro tracking

Log meals in a lightweight way and keep an eye on the nutrition details you care about most.

Movement

Exercise and workout tracking

Record workouts and exercise habits in the same place as the rest of your wellness routine.

Recovery

Sleep, weight, and water

Keep the daily basics together so trends are easier to spot over time.

Reflection

Diary and gratitude journaling

Use journaling to capture quick thoughts, notes, and the good parts of the day.

Food planning

Recipes with notes

Store recipes and add notes that help you stay organized and shop smarter.

Consistency

Built to stay simple

NewLeaf is intentionally lighter than feature-heavy wellness apps so it stays easy to use.

Subscription and access

NewLeaf uses a subscription model, with every feature unlocked while subscribed or after purchasing lifetime access.

Monthly subscription or lifetime access

Access to NewLeaf requires a monthly auto-renewing subscription. If you prefer not to subscribe, you can also purchase lifetime access. Either way, the full app is available while your access is active.

Why it exists

NewLeaf is for people who want a simpler wellness tool without the feeling that every routine needs a dashboard full of extra features.

Stay organized, reflect over time, and build a routine that fits your life

Whether you want to track one habit or your entire day, NewLeaf gives you a practical way to stay organized, notice useful patterns, and keep momentum without unnecessary clutter.
