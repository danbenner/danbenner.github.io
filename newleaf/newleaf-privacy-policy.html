NewLeaf Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 14, 2026

NewLeaf ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Data We Collect

  • User content you create in the app, including recipes, journal entries, and workouts.
  • Crash and diagnostics information provided by Apple to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

How We Use Data

  • To provide the app's features.
  • To store the information you create inside the app.
  • To sync supported content through Apple's services when enabled.
  • To diagnose crashes or stability issues.

Purchases and Subscriptions

If you buy a subscription or lifetime unlock, Apple processes the transaction and provides us with the purchase status needed to unlock paid features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Crash and Diagnostics Data

We may receive crash logs and diagnostics from Apple. We use this information only to improve app stability and fix bugs. We do not sell or share this data.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across third-party apps or websites for advertising purposes.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with Apple services when needed to deliver app functionality such as iCloud sync, in-app purchases, or system-provided diagnostics.

Contact

For privacy or legal questions, use the support contact listed for NewLeaf in the App Store.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above when changes are made.
